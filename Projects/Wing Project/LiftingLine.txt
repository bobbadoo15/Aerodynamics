Pressure:
    * Pressure difference is zero at the wingtips (upper/lower pressures converge)
    * There is no lift at the wingtips due to zero pressure difference
    * Near the wingtips, high pressure below the wing "spills" outward toward the tips
    * The spilled pressure moves around the wingtips and inward along the wingspan
Vortex Sheets:
    * An airfoil is created using vortex sheets where vortex strength varies along the 
        sheet
    * Vortex strengths do not vary along the wingspan at that point
    * Because high-pressure moves outward below the airfoil and low-pressure moves
        inward, the spanwise velocities are different; this creates a trailing vortex 
        sheet at the trailing edge
    * The trailing vortex sheet is not bound to any surface and moves freely; this
        sheet changes shapes downstream by the induced flow field
    * Cannot synthesize sheets perpendicular to the airfoil, the sheets can be
        synthesized in the form of a horseshoe-shaped filaments
Vortex Strength:
    * Vortex strength stays constant along the entire filament; the bound circulation
        distribution (circulation strengths for each filament at the TE) decreases along 
        the span
Wingspan:
    * Aerodynamic forces are constant if the chord length is constant throughout the 
        wingspan
Wingtip Vortices:
    * When the sheet completely rolls up from the lower aft surface of the airfoil at
        the wingtip
    * Potential flow predicts the vortices trail the wingtips while keeping the vortex
        strengths constant; viscous forces prevent this happening in reality
